How it works

You stay in control of your documents.

We use a two-step process to avoid collecting sensitive documents before we know whether we can help.

1

Submit an enquiry

Share your contact details, service and destination. No passport or confidential document is required at this stage.

2

Wait for our decision

Your enquiry is sent to our email. We review it manually and decide whether we can accept the request.

3

Upload only after approval

If approved, you receive a private, time-limited upload link by email. If we cannot help, we send a polite decline instead.

Before you apply

Keep your first enquiry simple. Do not send passport numbers, bank details or sensitive files in this form.

  • Choose one of the three services.
  • Select the destination country.
  • Provide a reachable email and phone number.
  • Wait for our approval before uploading documents.

Review policy: You may receive an approval or a polite decline. Submitting an enquiry does not confirm service availability.

About us

Clear communication. Responsible assistance.

AKONDO GLOBAL WORLD is being developed as a customer-focused travel and documentation assistance brand.

Who we are

AKONDO GLOBAL WORLD provides an online starting point for people seeking information and assistance related to visa applications, visa invitation enquiries and air-ticket arrangements. Each request is reviewed individually before any documents are requested.

Our mission

To make the first step of travel-related enquiries clearer, more accessible and more organised through respectful communication, transparent review and careful handling of personal information.

Our vision

To build a trusted, internationally accessible service experience that helps customers understand the next steps for their travel enquiry—without making unrealistic promises.

Our service principles

Human reviewEvery request is checked before acceptance.
Clear expectationsEnquiry is not a guarantee of service or approval.
Privacy firstDocuments are requested only after acceptance.
Respectful serviceCustomers receive a clear response either way.
